# At Your Door Painting & Home Renovation 🎨🏡

A clean, simple, and colorful one-page website for **At Your Door Painting** — on-site residential house painting & renovation services (*"Ghar jaakar paint aur renovation ka kaam"*).

> **Public GitHub Repository:** [https://github.com/Go-Execution/atyourdoorpainting](https://github.com/Go-Execution/atyourdoorpainting)

---

## 🌟 Simple & Playful Highlights

1. **🎨 Interactive Click-to-Splatter:** Click anywhere to burst colorful paint splatters on screen.
2. **🖌️ Interactive Room Colorizer:** Click any color swatch (Coral, Ocean Blue, Sage, Sunshine, Lavender, Slate) to watch an animated paint roller swipe across the wall and change the room color in real time.
3. **🛠️ 3 Simple Doorstep Services:**
   - **Interior Home Painting:** Walls, ceilings, clean laser edges, zero paint odor.
   - **Cabinets & Doors:** Ultra-smooth factory spray finish.
   - **Drywall & Renovation Fix:** Cracks, nail holes, and plaster smoothed like glass.
4. **↔️ Before & After Slider:** Interactive visual proof comparing scuffed old walls to a modern fresh painted room.
5. **📐 Quick 1-Slider Price Estimator:** Slide square footage for an instant cost and timeframe estimate.
6. **🎉 Booking Modal with Confetti:** One-click booking with instant celebratory confetti.

---

## 💻 Tech Stack
- HTML5, Tailwind CSS, Lucide Icons, Canvas-Confetti, Vanilla JS.
- No heavy build steps; open `index.html` directly in any browser.
